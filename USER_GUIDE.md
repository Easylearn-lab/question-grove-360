# Question Grove 360 - User Guide

## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. Visit the Question Grove 360 landing page
2. Click "Start Learning" or "Sign In"
3. Choose your preferred sign-in method:
   - Email/Password
   - Google OAuth
4. Complete your profile:
   - Medical specialty
   - Training year
   - Target exam
   - Country
   - Target exam date

### Setting Your Study Goals

After creating your account, set your study goals:

1. Go to Profile Settings
2. Select your target exam (USMLE, MRCGP, MRCP, etc.)
3. Set your target exam date
4. Choose your daily study goal (questions/time)
5. Select your preferred study mode

## Features Overview

### 1. Question Bank

The Question Bank contains thousands of medical exam questions organized by specialty and difficulty.

**How to Use:**
1. Navigate to "Question Bank"
2. Select a specialty (Cardiology, Respiratory, etc.)
3. Choose difficulty level (Easy, Medium, Hard)
4. Select study mode:
   - **Tutor Mode**: Immediate feedback after each question
   - **Exam Mode**: All questions at once, feedback at end

**Features:**
- Bookmark important questions
- Flag questions for review
- Add personal notes
- View explanations
- Track your accuracy

**Tips:**
- Start with Easy questions to build confidence
- Use Tutor Mode to learn
- Use Exam Mode to practice under pressure
- Review flagged questions regularly

### 2. Mock Exams

Full-length timed exams that simulate the real exam experience.

**How to Use:**
1. Navigate to "Mock Exams"
2. Select an exam
3. Click "Start Exam"
4. Answer all questions within the time limit
5. Review your results

**Features:**
- Real-time countdown timer
- Question navigation
- Flag questions for review
- Automatic submission when time expires
- Detailed score breakdown by specialty

**Tips:**
- Take mocks in a distraction-free environment
- Simulate real exam conditions
- Review all incorrect answers
- Track your progress over time

### 3. Note360 Study Notes

Comprehensive, high-yield study notes organized by specialty.

**How to Use:**
1. Navigate to "Note360"
2. Select a specialty
3. Browse or search for topics
4. Read notes with highlighted key points
5. Bookmark important sections

**Features:**
- High-yield highlights
- Organized by specialty
- Search functionality
- Bookmark sections
- Printable format

**Tips:**
- Read notes before tackling questions
- Focus on high-yield content
- Use as reference during question practice
- Print and review before exams

### 4. Pattern Recognition Flashcards

Spaced repetition flashcards for mastering key concepts.

**How to Use:**
1. Navigate to "Pattern Recognition"
2. Select a specialty
3. Study flashcards
4. Rate your confidence (Easy/Medium/Hard)
5. Track your progress

**Features:**
- 3D flip animation
- Spaced repetition algorithm
- Mastery level tracking
- Progress statistics
- Mobile-friendly swipe gestures

**Tips:**
- Study daily for best results
- Focus on "Learning" cards first
- Review "Reviewing" cards regularly
- Aim to master all cards

### 5. SCA AI Consultation Simulator

Practice clinical consultations with AI-powered patient roleplay.

**How to Use:**
1. Navigate to "SCA Simulator"
2. Select a case
3. Start consultation
4. Speak naturally to the AI patient
5. Receive feedback and scoring

**Features:**
- Real-time speech recognition
- AI patient responses
- Domain-specific scoring
- Detailed feedback
- Case history tracking

**Tips:**
- Speak clearly and naturally
- Take your time with responses
- Review feedback after each case
- Practice regularly for improvement

### 6. AI Coach360

Your personal AI study assistant available from any page.

**How to Use:**
1. Click the chat icon (bottom-right)
2. Ask questions or request help
3. Receive personalized recommendations
4. Get study strategies and tips

**Features:**
- Context-aware responses
- Performance analysis
- Study recommendations
- Exam strategies
- Motivation and support

**Tips:**
- Ask specific questions
- Share your study challenges
- Request personalized study plans
- Use for motivation and support

### 7. Dashboard

Your personal study dashboard with key metrics.

**How to Use:**
1. Navigate to Dashboard after login
2. View your study statistics
3. Check your study streak
4. Review upcoming exams
5. Track your progress

**Features:**
- Study streak tracking
- Accuracy trends
- Pass probability gauge
- Daily goals
- Upcoming exam schedule

**Tips:**
- Check daily for motivation
- Set realistic daily goals
- Track your improvement
- Celebrate milestones

## Tips for Success

### Study Strategy
1. **Assess Your Baseline**
   - Take a diagnostic mock exam
   - Identify weak areas

2. **Create a Study Plan**
   - Allocate time per specialty
   - Set daily goals
   - Schedule mock exams

3. **Active Learning**
   - Use Tutor Mode first
   - Then practice with Exam Mode
   - Review all incorrect answers

4. **Spaced Repetition**
   - Use Pattern Recognition daily
   - Review weak areas regularly
   - Space out your study sessions

5. **Practice Under Pressure**
   - Take mock exams regularly
   - Simulate real exam conditions
   - Track your progress

### Time Management
- Study 2-3 hours daily for best results
- Dedicate 30 minutes to flashcards
- Take 1-2 mock exams per week
- Review for 30 minutes daily

### Motivation
- Track your progress daily
- Celebrate small wins
- Use AI Coach for support
- Join study groups (coming soon)
