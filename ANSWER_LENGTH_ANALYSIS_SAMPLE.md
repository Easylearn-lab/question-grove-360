## Analysis Notes

1. **Common patterns in oversized answers:**
   - Excessive elaboration and sub-clauses that belong in the explanation column
   - Long medication names that could be abbreviated (e.g., "intravenous immunoglobulin (IVIG)" → "IVIG")
   - Multiple treatment options listed when only the primary should be in the option
   - Clinical context that duplicates the question stem

2. **Proposed shortening strategy:**
   - Keep the core clinical answer (diagnosis, treatment, or finding)
   - Remove elaboration, secondary options, and contextual detail
   - Use abbreviations consistently (STEMI, IVIG, ARB, etc.)
   - Maintain clinical accuracy while reducing verbosity

3. **Quality assurance:**
   - All proposed versions retain the essential clinical information
   - No medical accuracy is compromised
   - Explanation columns will carry the full detail
   - Options now follow the 8-20 word guideline
